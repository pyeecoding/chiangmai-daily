Haze season: Chiang Mai's smoke problem is usually worst from roughly late January to April, peaking around March, driven by agricultural and forest burning in the region and atmospheric inversions that trap PM2.5 in the valley. Burning can start in late autumn, so a rise in the table before the main season is possible.
